Jaemyeong Lee

I am a M.S. candidate in the Machine Intelligence Lab (MILAB) at Jeonbuk National University, supervised by Prof. Sang Jun Lee.

My research interests include 3D perception for autonomous driving, robotic vision, and VR/AR applications.

Currently, I am working on omnidirectional stereo matching, with a focus on enabling robust 3D perception of surrounding environments using multi-fisheye camera systems.
